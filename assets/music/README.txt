Drop music files (.ogg, .mp3 or .wav) into these folders and the game plays them:

  music/title/     the title screen
  music/day/       daytime
  music/night/     after 8 PM
  music/tavern/    inside the tavern

  ambience/day/    birds, wind (plays quietly under the music)
  ambience/night/  crickets
  ambience/tavern/ fireplace, chatter

Several files in one folder play one after another in a random order.
An empty music folder borrows the daytime music. See scripts/music.gd.
